# EUDR TARIC document codes

The 15 document codes that go on an EU customs declaration for goods under the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR, Regulation (EU) 2023/1115): C716, C717, C718, C720, C725 and the Y codes for exemptions.

Files:

- [`codes.json`](codes.json): each code with a plain-language meaning and whether it is used at import, export or both, plus the source and the date it was checked.
- [`codes.csv`](codes.csv): the same table as CSV.

Explained with examples (which code applies to you, import and export differences): **[EUDR TARIC document codes on the EU Rules Finder](https://eurules.vedacta.com/eudr/taric-document-codes)**. Is your goods code in Annex I at all? The free [EUDR code checker](https://eurules.vedacta.com/eudr) answers.

## Source

European Commission, DG Taxation and Customs Union: [TARIC data created for Regulation (EU) 2023/1115](https://circabc.europa.eu/ui/group/0e5f18c2-4b2f-42e9-aed4-dfe50ae1263b/library/17035dd6-15c9-41bb-8bcb-68d2c8169b21/details) (Ares(2024)6896255), Revision 1 of 23 September 2026. Checked on 19 October 2026.

The meanings are our summaries, not the official wording. On the day you declare, the measure in the EU's [TARIC consultation](https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/taric/taric_consultation.jsp) for your goods code is what counts. Rules apply from 30 December 2026, and from 30 June 2027 for micro and small operators established by 31 December 2024.
